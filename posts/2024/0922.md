人为什么要结婚

Why do people get married

难道就为了一起搭伙吃饭

just to share a meal together?

为了一起还房贷

To pay the mortgage together?

为了生个孩子 然后给孩子当牛做马

To have a baby and then act as a cow and horse for the child?

不是这样的

That’s not true

因为人生来孤独

Because people are born alone

却又渴望拥有长久的陪伴

But also eager for long-term companionship

因为所有有风险的事情

Because everything that carries risks

都需要领个证才能去做

You need to obtain a certificate before you can do it

比如开车、当医生，当然也包括爱情

Such as driving, being a doctor, and of course, love

There is someone among the countless lights waiting for you to come home

还因为那个人是他

Because that person is him

所以就算知道要一辈子

So even if I knew it would take a lifetime

Facing the same face is terrifying

But still eagerly waiting

要押上余生去跟命运赌一把

want to risk the rest of my life to gamble with fate

The ”I do“ at the wedding means:

在这路遥马急的人间

In this busy and inconstant world

You got me completely
