> <https://www.ipify.org/>

- 1、IPv4 请求url

```js
https://api.ipify.org?format=json
```

响应类型：json

输出示例：{"ip":"98.207.254.136"}

- 2、IPv4/IPv6 请求url

```js
https://api64.ipify.org?format=json
```

响应类型：json

输出示例：{"ip":"98.207.254.136"} or {"ip":"2a00:1450:400f:80d::200e"}
