从现在开始

From now on

你仅仅需要在乎三件事

You only need to care about three things

你自己

Yourself

你的钱

Your money

和你内心的平静

And your inner peace

生命周期中的低潮阶段只是短暂的

Life-cycle undercurrents are only temporary

你正在死去

You are dying

但也将迎来重生

But also will welcome rebirth

不要去偷窥别人的生活

Don't pry into other people's lives

也不要去揣测别人的想法

Nor attempt to speculate about others' thoughts

更不要陷在过去担忧未来

Don't dwell in the past, worrying about the future

任何引起你内耗的是人和事

Anything and anyone that causes internal strife for you

多想一下都没必要

It's not worth thinking about any further

把时间和精力还给自己

Return your time and energy to yourself

在声色名利中守住本心

Stay true to yourself amidst fame, wealth, and pleasure

从容面对世俗目光，悠然前行

Walk confidently amongst the scrutiny of the secular world

学会独立生活吧

Learn to live independently

不要对别人心存太多期待

Don't place too many expectations on others

我们总是在不断地寻求

We always seek to find

能为自己分担痛苦和悲伤的人

those who can share our pain and sorrow

然而大多数情况下

Yet, most of the time

我们那些惊天动地的悲伤

our earth-shattering sorrows

在别人眼里

are in others' eyes

不过是随手拂过的尘埃

but mere specks of dust brushed aside
